THE PROJECT

Modernizing the school's online presence

Bible Truth Ministries Academy needed a redesigned website that better represented the school and made important information easier for prospective and current families to find.

I redesigned the existing WordPress website, restructuring content, rebuilding key pages, improving navigation, and creating responsive layouts for desktop, tablet, and mobile. The project progressed from design and content planning through testing, stakeholder revisions, deployment, and production launch.

Project Scope

  • Website redesign
  • Information architecture
  • Responsive design
  • WordPress customization
  • Content organization
  • Donation functionality
  • Testing & deployment

THE REDESIGN

Creating a clearer, more engaging experience

The redesign focused on giving the school a stronger visual identity while making the website easier for families to navigate and use across devices.

01

Homepage & Visual Identity

Reworked the homepage to establish a stronger first impression through updated typography, school imagery, clearer messaging, and prominent calls to action for prospective families.

02

Content & User Journey

Reorganized key information around the needs of prospective families, creating clearer paths through Admissions, Academics, Student Life, and campus visits. Calls to action guide families from learning about the school toward scheduling a tour and beginning the admissions process.

03

Responsive Experience

Adapted navigation, typography, imagery, spacing, and content layouts across desktop, tablet, and mobile. Rather than simply scaling the desktop design down, key sections were adjusted to preserve readability, hierarchy, and calls to action on smaller screens.

CHALLENGES & SOLUTIONS

Solving problems beyond the initial design

01

Responsive Design

Complex hero sections and page layouts needed to work across desktop, tablet, and mobile. I adjusted typography, spacing, imagery, and layout behavior at different breakpoints to preserve the site's visual hierarchy and usability.

02

Content Organization

School information needed clearer structure for prospective families. I reorganized content and calls to action to create more direct paths through admissions, academics, student life, and campus visits.

03

Deployment & Production

Moving the redesigned site from staging to production required troubleshooting deployment and media issues. I tested the live site after launch and resolved issues to ensure the finished experience remained consistent.

OUTCOME

From redesign to production

The redesigned website was successfully launched as Bible Truth Ministries Academy's production site, giving the school a more modern, responsive online presence and clearer paths for families exploring the school.
